# NM AI HPC Design Philosophy

1. **Mission before market.** Commercial utilization strengthens sovereign and research capability rather than displacing it.
2. **Policy before placement.** Identity, authority, provenance and data classification are resolved before compute admission.
3. **Fabric before fleet.** Interconnect topology is a first-class part of the computer.
4. **Sovereignty through federation.** Institutions retain ownership and control while approved excess capacity can participate.
5. **Move compute toward protected data.** Compute federation does not imply uncontrolled data movement.
6. **Zero Trust end to end.** Network location alone grants nothing.
7. **Measure everything.** Utilization, allocation, latency, energy, security and cost are observable.
8. **Human authority governs machine optimization.** AI can optimize schedules inside policy, not silently redefine policy.
9. **Open interfaces over captivity.** Preserve portability of workloads, accounting and governance data.
10. **Design for expansion.** Establish interfaces that can grow from one production domain to a multi-site statewide fabric.

> Build enough compute to matter. Build enough fabric to make it move. Build enough governance to trust it. Build enough openness to let New Mexico grow around it.
