# Avanyu Human-Governed Agentic Architecture

**Doctrine version: 2026-09-12**

## Prime directive
**AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.**

Avanyu operates specialist AI agents as bounded members of a human-governed delivery system. The control plane is:

**Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client**

## Authority model
0. **Observe** — telemetry and evidence only.
1. **Recommend** — findings and proposed actions.
2. **Generate** — code, configurations and change artifacts without execution.
3. **Human-approved execution** — specific authorized human approval required.
4. **Pre-authorized autonomous remediation** — only narrow, reversible, low-blast-radius actions with logging and rollback.

No agent has unlimited standing authority.

## Free-agent roster
- SOC-01 · Picarus Sentinel — defensive telemetry, threat modeling and bounded remediation.
- DEV-01 · Avanyu Core — architecture and software/infrastructure generation.
- SEC-02 · NIST/PCI Auditor — evidence mapping, gap analysis and POA&M support.
- OPS-01 · Platform Engineering — Terraform, containers, observability and controlled change.
- COM-01 · ZIAWOLF Media — communications and visualization under human review.

## Evidence plane
Agent context retrieval, recommendations, generated artifacts, approvals, executions and rollback events are evidence-producing activities. RAG context is not authority. Consequential decisions must remain traceable to policy, evidence and an accountable human.

## Picarus rule
Picarus is a privileged defensive specialist, not the sovereign authority. Missing immutable recovery evidence elevates risk. Remediation follows empirical telemetry. Automated changes are constrained by authorization tier, reversibility, mission impact and auditability.

## Commercial model
Avanyu sells Advisory Hours, Architecture Sprints, Assessments, Managed Services and Fixed Deliverables. Cloud/model consumption is separately metered, included transparently or passed through. AI processing time is not represented as human professional-service time.

## Market position
**Enterprise architecture without enterprise overhead.** Human-accountable AI agents perform bounded research, engineering, security, compliance and operational analysis under a unified evidence and authorization plane.
