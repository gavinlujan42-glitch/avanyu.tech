AVANYU TRUST PLANE · CANONICAL PUBLIC DOCTRINE

AI supplies velocity.
People retain authority.

Avanyu uses bounded specialist agents to multiply research, engineering, security, compliance and operational capacity. Consequential authority remains human-owned.

Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client

Authorization tiers

Tier 0 · Observe — Read telemetry and evidence only.
Tier 1 · Recommend — Produce findings and proposed actions.
Tier 2 · Generate — Produce code, policy, configuration or change artifacts without execution.
Tier 3 · Human-approved execution — Execute only after an authorized person approves the specific change.
Tier 4 · Bounded autonomy — Only explicitly pre-authorized, reversible, low-blast-radius actions with logging and rollback.

Evidence before assertion

Prompts, retrieval context, proposed changes, approvals, execution results, rollback events and compliance evidence are evidence-producing activities. RAG context informs decisions but does not grant authority.

Picarus Sentinel

Picarus is a privileged defensive specialist, not the control-plane authority. Missing immutable recovery evidence elevates risk. Remediation follows empirical telemetry and the applicable authorization tier. The agent cannot silently expand its own privileges.

Commercial clarity

Professional services are packaged as advisory hours, architecture sprints, assessments, managed services or fixed deliverables. Cloud and model consumption is separately metered, transparently included or passed through. Machine processing time is not represented as human professional-service labor.

Enterprise architecture without enterprise overhead. Human-accountable AI agents operate under a unified authorization and evidence plane.
