New Mexico Impact

The highest-consequence pathways are telecommunications compromise, identity takeover, exposed edge systems, IT-to-OT movement, vendor/supply-chain access and ransomware-driven interruption. Water and wastewater deserve particular attention because Iranian-affiliated actors have targeted U.S. PLC environments, while PRC actors have demonstrated strategic interest in U.S. critical infrastructure.

Zia Wolf Defensive Priorities

Phishing-resistant MFA for privileged/high-value users; hardened identity recovery; rapid internet-facing asset remediation; IT/OT segmentation and manual operating capability; carrier/vendor trust validation; immutable recovery; help-desk identity verification; contractor and remote-worker proofing; and behavior-based hunting mapped to ATT&CK.
